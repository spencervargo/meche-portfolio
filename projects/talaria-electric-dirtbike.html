Talaria Electric Dirtbike CAD

Completely designed a Talaria electric dirt bike in 3D CAD using SOLIDWORKS, with emphasis on accurate mechanical geometry, suspension kinematics, and realistic wheel dynamics.

Overview

For my final project in Computer Aided Design, I replicated the Talaria electric dirt bike as a full assembly in SOLIDWORKS. The goal was to demonstrate geometric and mechanical accuracy, so I used a tape measure and ruler for reference while modeling the frame, swingarm, steering head, triple clamps, wheels, tires, and brake rotors. To capture realistic suspension motion, I applied mates and reference geometry, and I learned to use limit mates to achieve mechanical accuracy when simulating the front fork and rear linkage through their travel.

Mechanical Properties and Methods

  • Mass properties approximated via material assignments and custom mass for COTS components.
  • Center of gravity and moment of inertia evaluated at assembly level for ride feel estimation.
  • Suspension kinematics explored with configuration‑driven positions and mate controllers.
  • Clearance checks across full travel and steering sweep to de‑risk packaging.
