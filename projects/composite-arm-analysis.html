Crawler Robotics Platform

Team-built crawler combining 3D‑printed parts, laser‑cut wood, and off‑the‑shelf hardware. Fully modeled and assembled in SOLIDWORKS, including an animation to validate motion, clearances, and assembly order.

Overview

We designed and built a small four‑wheel crawler focused on manufacturability and rapid iteration. The chassis and structural members were laser‑cut from plywood and acrylic for speed and repeatability, while brackets, spacers, and complex interfaces were 3D‑printed. The full assembly was modeled in SOLIDWORKS with accurate mates to simulate suspension compliance, steering range, and drivetrain packaging. An animation demonstrated assembly sequence and operational motion, de‑risking interference before fabrication.

Teamwork and Organization

  • Defined work breakdown structure (CAD, fabrication, electronics, test) and weekly milestones.
  • Used part numbering and configuration naming conventions to keep SOLIDWORKS assemblies organized.
  • Owned the assembly master file; merged teammates’ subassemblies via Pack‑and‑Go and design tables.
  • Created a build of materials (BOM) and fabrication drawings for laser cutting and 3D printing.
