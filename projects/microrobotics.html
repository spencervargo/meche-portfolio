Microrobotics Research Assistant

Engineered a functional microrobot smaller than 20 mm in the Magnetic Microsystems & Microrobotics Lab - Prof. Camilo Velez Cuervo.

Overview

This project focused on coding, researching, and assembling a microrobot with a footprint around 20 mm. I implemented an embedded control loop that maps accelerometer readings to precise motor commands. Future iterations will be capable of following a gradient autonomously.

Responsibilities

  • Obtain parts through UCI purchase requests.
  • Program the control system in Arduino IDE to translate accelerometer data into motor commands.
  • Solder COTS motors onto the main board.
  • Research the board schematics and data sheets to understand port configurations and bit-level data flow.

Technologies

  • Arduino
  • Embedded C/C++
  • IMU/Accelerometer
  • Microsoldering
